How the script uses a preset

    
    
      
  • A preset is a wish list. Lines the bank doesn't have are skipped, logged once and remembered for the run, so they don't send it back to the bank.
    • 
      
  • Worn lines are put on first. A worn line with several items takes the first one the bank has (best first).
    • 
      
  • All means the whole stack is withdrawn. Powerbursts are withdrawn last, so they're the first to be cut if the backpack fills up.
    • 
      
  • One backpack slot is left free for the clue scroll (two when a sealed stack is banked). Clue items of the tier are never banked.
    • 
      
  • With Use Meerkats on, the bank trip also takes a Meerkats pouch and all Meerkat scrolls, and summons them at the bank.
    • 
      
  • Per-account edits come from the settings' Presets tab and are saved to . Tiers with an edit show a dot on their tab when that account is picked.
